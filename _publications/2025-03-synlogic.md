We propose SynLogic, a framework for synthesizing verifiable reasoning data at scale, which significantly improves logical reasoning capabilities in large language models. Our approach generates complex logical reasoning problems with verified solutions, enabling models to learn more robust reasoning patterns through reinforcement learning.
